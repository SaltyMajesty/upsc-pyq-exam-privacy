Privacy Policy

UPSC PYQ Mock Test · com.upscpyq.exampro
Last updated: 21 October 2026

This app does not collect any data. It works entirely offline. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted, stored on a server, or shared with anyone.

Information we collect

None. UPSC PYQ Mock Test does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal or sensitive user data.

Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Photos, files, contacts, or any other content on your device
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostic information

There is no account, no sign-in, and no registration of any kind.

Internet access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and has no network capability. It cannot send or receive data even in principle. All question papers and answer keys are bundled inside the app and read from local storage.

Permissions

The app requests no Android runtime permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, storage, contacts, or any other protected feature of your device.

Your exam attempts

Answers you select during a paper are held in the device's memory only while that paper is open, so the app can score it. They are not written to disk and are discarded when you leave the paper or close the app. The app keeps no history of past attempts.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking libraries of any kind. No data is shared with third parties, because no data is collected.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for candidates preparing for the Union Public Service Commission Civil Services Examination and is not directed at children. Since the app collects no data from any user, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting data — for example, if optional crash reporting is added in a future version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after such an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
