Privacy Policy

UPSC PYQ Mock Test · com.upscpyq.exampro
Last updated: 22 October 2026

We do not ask you for any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no name, email address or phone number collected by us. Your exam answers stay on your own device.

The app does use an internet connection, and it includes advertising and in-app purchases. Those services — all operated by Google or by RevenueCat — receive some technical information about your device. This page explains exactly what, and why.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We do not operate a server that stores anything about you. We never collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other file on your device, and the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting library.

What stays on your device

The app saves a small amount of information in its own private storage so it behaves sensibly between sessions:

  • An unfinished paper — which questions you have answered, which you flagged for review, where you had reached, and the remaining time on a mock test — so you can resume it later
  • Your language choice, English or Hindi
  • Your light or dark theme preference
  • Whether you hold an ad-free pass, and when it expires

This never leaves your device and is not visible to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app erases all of it.

Advertising

A short rewarded advert can play before a paper begins. Adverts are served by Google AdMob, which is a separate company from us and handles the advert itself.

To do that, AdMob receives technical information from your device, including your Android advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, and whether you viewed or interacted with an advert. It may use this to limit repeated adverts, to measure them, and — where you have allowed it — to personalise them.

Where the law requires consent, the app shows Google's consent form the first time you open it, and your choice is respected. You can change that choice at any time from Ad privacy choices on the app's home screen. Taking an ad-free pass stops adverts loading at all.

How Google handles this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and in Google's AdMob documentation.

The ad-free pass

The optional ad-free pass is sold through Google Play Billing and managed with RevenueCat, which keeps track of whether your pass is still valid.

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your card details. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an anonymous identifier the app generates for your install, and basic device and country information, so that your purchase can be recognised and restored. It is not linked to a name or an email address, because the app never asks for one.

See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

App settings

The question papers are stored inside the app in encrypted form, and the app fetches the setting needed to open them from Firebase Remote Config, a Google service. This is also how we correct a setting without releasing a new version.

Making that request sends Google a Firebase installation identifier along with your IP address and basic app and device information. It does not contain anything about your answers or your results. Because the app needs this to open a paper, a connection is required.

Internet and permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions — it never asks for your camera, microphone, location, storage or contacts. The permissions it declares are the ones the advertising, billing and configuration services need in order to work:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE
  • AD_ID and the Android AdServices permissions, used by AdMob
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE and WAKE_LOCK, used while an advert is playing

Children's privacy

The app is made for candidates preparing for the Civil Services Examination and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child. If you believe a child has used the app and you want its locally stored data removed, clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything.

Your choices

  • Adverts: change your consent from Ad privacy choices in the app, or remove adverts entirely with an ad-free pass
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads
  • Everything stored locally: clear the app's data or uninstall the app

Because we hold no data about you on any server of ours, there is nothing for us to delete on request. For data held by Google or RevenueCat, follow the links above.

Changes to this policy

If the app begins using data in a way this page does not describe, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
